The original license is retained in [vendor/hand-drawn/LICENSE](vendor/hand-drawn/LICENSE); file hashes are in PACKAGE-MANIFEST.json and the upstream revision is recorded in UPSTREAM.json. All 21 recipes are callable without rewriting their visual rules; the two memory-album presets remain available. Reference-cache fallback files come from immutable pre-migration revision `cdfd004c89164096c861ad06ebab2aa1527d1710` and must match the current manifest's exact size and SHA-256. The local integration does not claim upstream validation for newly generated photos.
