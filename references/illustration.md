# Illustration

Use `photo_style: illustration` for a clean, playful editorial illustration.

Use confident dark contours, simple shapes, calm fields of color and selective detail. Keep the original subject, scene and emotional read recognizable rather than replacing it with generic clip art. Preserve every person's pose, clothing and accessories. Expand scenery only to fill the resolved ratio.

Flat-master prompt anchor: “Edit the supplied photo as a refined, playful editorial illustration with clean contours and flat color. Preserve recognizable people, poses, clothing, accessories and source landmarks. Extend scenery only. Output one flat, full-bleed {image_ratio} canvas. No book, frame, crease, border, captions or watermark.”
