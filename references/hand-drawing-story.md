# Hand-drawing Story

Use `photo_style: hand-drawing-story` for minimal, narrative line-and-wash artwork.

Use delicate hand-drawn ink lines, sparse watercolor accents and intentional negative space. Reduce busy detail while retaining the specific people, animals, objects and setting that make the source photograph meaningful. Preserve every person's recognizable pose, clothing and accessories. Expand scenery only to fill the resolved ratio.

Flat-master prompt anchor: “Edit the supplied photo as a minimal hand-drawn story: delicate ink lines, sparse watercolor accents and generous intentional negative space. Preserve recognizable people, poses, clothing, accessories and key source details. Extend scenery only. Output one flat, full-bleed {image_ratio} canvas. No book, frame, crease, border, captions or watermark.”
