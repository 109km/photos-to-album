# Pencil

Use `photo_style: pencil` for rich colored-pencil travel artwork.

Build forms from visible pencil strokes, soft crosshatching, varied pressure and real paper tooth. Keep colors grounded in the original photo; favor texture and observation over a digital airbrush finish. Preserve every person's recognizable face, pose, clothing and accessories. Expand scenery only to fill the resolved ratio.

Flat-master prompt anchor: “Edit the supplied photo into a tactile colored-pencil illustration on fine-grain warm paper. Preserve recognizable people, poses, clothing, accessories and the source composition. Extend scenery only. Output one flat, full-bleed {image_ratio} canvas. No book, frame, crease, border, captions or watermark.”

Use the shared travel-album book renderer unchanged.
